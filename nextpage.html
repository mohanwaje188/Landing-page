Business growth refers to the expansion of a company’s operations, revenue, and market share over time. It can occur through various strategies, such as increasing sales, entering new markets, or launching new products. A growing business typically aims to scale its operations to meet rising demand, improve efficiency, and achieve long-term profitability. The share market, on the other hand, is a platform where businesses raise capital by selling shares of ownership to the public. Investors purchase these shares, becoming partial owners of the company. As a business grows and performs well, its stock value tends to increase, providing returns to shareholders. Conversely, poor performance can cause the stock price to drop. Business growth and the share market are closely connected, as a thriving company often leads to a rise in its stock value, attracting more investors and supporting further expansion.
